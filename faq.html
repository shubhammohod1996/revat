What is Revat Fund?
Revat is developing a platform where eligible investors select from assessed crypto traders and allocate through a proposed exchange-subaccount and risk management framework.
How much capital is proposed to start?
The business draft proposes a starting minimum of 10,000 USDT, allocating at least 1,000 USDT to each of a minimum of ten traders. These are proposed terms, not a live offer.
Do investors choose their own traders?
Under the intended model, investors select approved traders from available platform listings. Approval does not predict returns.
Where does capital remain?
In eligible exchange subaccounts owned by the investor at a supported exchange, not in a pooled Revat trading wallet. Exchange custody and trading risks remain.
Which exchanges are contemplated?
The business draft names Binance and Bybit, subject to exchange eligibility, supported account types, jurisdictions and final integration.
How does performance sharing work?
Indicative standard terms propose a 60% investor and 40% Revat share of eligible trading profits. Terms, calculations, costs and settlement are not final; losses are possible.
Can investors withdraw at any time?
Withdrawal and reallocation procedures, including handling of open positions and exchange restrictions, must be defined in final operating terms. No immediate-access promise is made.
Do I need an existing live track record?
No suitable live record is required for the proposed two-month demo assessment route. All applicants remain subject to approval.
Does passing assessment guarantee capital?
No. Approval creates eligibility for investor selection, not a guaranteed allocation or earnings.
How are traders paid?
The draft indicates trader compensation may be funded from Revat’s performance share, with preliminary percentages of approximately 20–25% of eligible generated profits and other terms possible at larger capital tiers.
What does the read-only API permit?
Read-only assessment access is intended to allow review of trading information without granting trading or withdrawal authority.
Does Revat guarantee profits?
No. Crypto trading can produce substantial losses, including loss of all allocated capital. No fixed yield or guaranteed return is offered.
Does the RMS guarantee losses stay under 10%?
No. The intended intervention target is for an individual trading subaccount, and execution conditions or system failures can produce losses beyond it.
Can Revat withdraw through the trading API?
The proposed setup requires trading-only API permissions with withdrawals disabled. Trading permissions still expose funds to trading risk.
Does investing across ten traders eliminate risk?
No. Strategies can move together, and correlated losses may occur across multiple trader allocations.
Is Revat available or regulated in my country?
Eligibility, legal entity details and regulatory status have not been confirmed in the supplied materials. They must be reviewed and published before live participation.
