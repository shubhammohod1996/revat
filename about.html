ABOUT REVAT FUND

Opportunity should be shaped by ability, choice and discipline.

We are developing an approach that brings assessed crypto trading talent and investor-selected capital into a more transparent, structured process.

Explore our model Contact Revat
OUR PURPOSE

Connecting talent and capital through a considered process.

Capable trading talent exists beyond large institutional desks. Investors, meanwhile, may seek clearer ways to review the people and strategies involved in trading their capital.

Revat is designed to bring discovery, verification, investor selection and monitoring into one connected model — with risk recognized at each step.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Talent. Capital. Discipline.

Talent should be demonstrated. Capital should be allocated deliberately. Risk should be acknowledged, measured and addressed through defined processes.

OUR VISION

Broader opportunity through informed participation.

To develop a digital asset trading ecosystem where proven ability can gain visibility and eligible investors can make clearer trader selection decisions.

OUR MISSION

Connect the right process to the right opportunity.

To build a structured platform for crypto trader assessment, investor-led capital allocation, performance visibility and defined risk management.

WHAT GUIDES OUR DESIGN

A modern experience with serious underlying principles.

01

Meaningful assessment

Trading ability is evaluated through evidence or a structured demo assessment, not marketing claims.

02

Deliberate choice

Investor selection is part of the proposed allocation process.

03

Risk awareness

Risk management is a necessary discipline, not a promise that losses cannot happen.

DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY

From a proof of concept to a broader platform vision.

Revat's business draft describes an initial proof of concept. Its next priorities include completing operational definitions, validating supported exchange connections, finalizing risk and fee terms, and making onboarding appropriate for the jurisdictions served.

The proof of concept has not been independently audited for this website content and must not be represented as proof of future returns or scaled performance.
