WHY TRADING TALENT MATTERS

Skill takes work. Access to capital takes opportunity.

Developing a trading approach requires observation, testing, experience and discipline. Revat aims to create a pathway for capable crypto traders to demonstrate what they can do and become eligible for investor selection.

Assessment establishes eligibility for consideration — not a promise of a funded account, a particular allocation or income.

PERFORMANCE & COMPENSATION

Potential compensation. Never guaranteed.

The preliminary model proposes trader profit shares of approximately 20–25% of eligible generated profits under standard arrangements, with approximately 5–15% possible for larger capital tiers.

Actual compensation depends on eligibility, investor allocations, trading results and signed commercial terms. A larger allocation does not guarantee earnings.
