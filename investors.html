THE ACCOUNT MODEL

Capital stays in eligible investor-owned exchange accounts.

Revat proposes separately managed trading allocations connected to supported Binance or Bybit subaccounts. This does not mean Revat or the exchanges guarantee capital security.

01

Exchange custody

Assets remain held by the supported exchange in accounts owned by the investor. Exchange and custody risk remain.

02

Restricted trading API

The proposed connection enables agreed trading activity, with API withdrawals disabled; trading losses remain possible.

03

Account-level risk control

The intended RMS intervention target is an individual subaccount threshold, not a guaranteed portfolio drawdown limit.

COMMERCIAL MODEL

Results shared under agreed terms.

Under the preliminary standard terms in Revat's business draft, investors would retain 60% of profits eligible for sharing, while Revat would receive 40%. Trader compensation comes from Revat's share.

For an individual allocation above 1 million USDT, the Revat share may reduce to approximately 25%, leaving around 75% for the investor, if agreed. All terms remain indicative.

The exact profit definition, high-water-mark or loss recovery, fees, settlement timing and other costs must be confirmed in final agreements.

PARTICIPATION & RISKS

An opportunity for investors who understand crypto trading risk.

You may be interested if you:

  • Want to evaluate and select individual trading talent.
  • Understand that performance may be volatile or negative.
  • Can bear the loss of the capital allocated.
  • Meet the eventual exchange and jurisdictional eligibility requirements.

No fixed yield, guaranteed return or assured 10% loss ceiling is offered. Trade execution, leverage, exchange incidents and correlated strategies may result in losses.
