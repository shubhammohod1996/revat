ACCOUNT STRUCTURE

Exchange-held assets. Separately managed allocations.

Under the proposed model, investor capital stays in eligible exchange subaccounts owned by the investor, rather than entering a shared Revat trading wallet.

Revat would execute agreed trading activity through appropriately permissioned connections. This does not make assets self-custodied or immune to exchange risk.

THE RMS

Understand the intended 10% intervention threshold.

The RMS is designed to intervene before losses reach 10% of an individual trading subaccount’s equity. It is not a guaranteed stop, insurance or portfolio-wide limit.

01 / SCOPE

Individual subaccount

The target applies to a particular trading subaccount, not automatically to the entire investor portfolio.

02 / EXECUTION

Real conditions matter

Gaps, slippage, liquidity constraints, liquidation and exchange outages can cause losses beyond the intended threshold.

03 / DEFINITION

Final rules are pending

The calculation basis, measurement period, unrealized P&L treatment and intervention behavior must be confirmed.

MATERIAL RISKS

Understand what can still go wrong.

The presence of multiple traders, restricted APIs and automated controls does not remove exposure to loss.

MARKET

Price and volatility risk

Digital asset prices may move rapidly, affecting both open positions and execution prices.

STRATEGY

Trader and correlation risk

Strategies can underperform, and many traders may experience losses simultaneously.

EXECUTION

Liquidity and slippage

Orders may fill later or at less favorable prices than expected, or fail to execute.

LEVERAGE

Liquidation risk

Where leverage is allowed, adverse moves can amplify losses and cause rapid liquidation.

TECHNOLOGY

API and system interruptions

Authentication, software, connectivity or exchange outages can interrupt execution and risk actions.

CUSTODY

Exchange and counterparty risk

Assets held at an exchange remain exposed to exchange operations, security and counterparty events.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Transparency starts with the right questions.

Potential participants should understand what access Revat receives, which exchange holds the assets, how profits and losses are calculated, and which contractual terms govern allocations.

Regulatory status, jurisdictional eligibility and service availability must also be verified before onboarding.

Read frequently asked questions

Before participation, confirm:

  • The correct legal entity and regulatory disclosures
  • Eligibility in your country of residence
  • Applicable fees and profit calculations
  • Exchange subaccount and API permissions
  • Withdrawal and reallocation procedures
  • Full RMS behavior and risk acknowledgments
