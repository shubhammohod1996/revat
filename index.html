RISK & ACCOUNT STRUCTURE

Risk matters.
So does how you address it.

Revat's proposed approach uses investor-owned exchange subaccounts and restricted trading API permissions. Its RMS is intended to intervene before an individual subaccount reaches a configured 10% equity-loss threshold.

That target is not a guarantee or a portfolio-wide loss limit. Trading losses, slippage, liquidations, API interruptions and exchange risks remain possible.

Understand the risk framework
THE PROPOSED ACCOUNT MODEL

Designed for more defined permissions.

Investor-owned exchange accounts

Capital remains with the supported exchange, subject to its custody risks.

Trading access, not withdrawal permission

Trading-only API keys are intended to have withdrawal permissions disabled.

Intervention-oriented RMS

Configured risk measures cannot guarantee a maximum trading loss.

THE ESSENTIALS

Understand the model before you participate.

01 / TRADER SELECTION

What does approval mean?

It creates eligibility for investors to consider a trader; it does not guarantee a capital allocation.

02 / EXCHANGE ACCOUNTS

Where does capital remain?

In the investor's eligible exchange subaccounts, rather than a pooled Revat trading wallet.

03 / RISK CONTROLS

Is the 10% threshold guaranteed?

No. It is an intended intervention target on an individual subaccount, not a loss ceiling.

04 / INVESTOR CHOICE

Can traders lose together?

Yes. Multiple allocations do not remove correlation risk or prevent portfolio losses.

05 / PARTICIPATION

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Results depend on trading performance and market conditions; losses are possible.

View all frequently asked questions
Does Revat guarantee returns?
No. Crypto trading involves substantial risk, including potential loss of capital. No fixed or assured returns are offered.
Can Revat withdraw assets through the API?
The proposed API setup disables withdrawal permission, but trading-only access can still lead to substantial losses.
Does trader approval guarantee funding?
No. Approved traders become eligible for selection. Actual allocation depends on investor choice and availability.
Is the RMS an absolute 10% loss limit?
No. The intended individual subaccount intervention threshold is not a guaranteed ceiling. Execution and market events may result in greater losses.
