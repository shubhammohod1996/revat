THE FOUR-PART PROCESS

A clear sequence. Meaningful choices.

The model connects trader assessment and investor selection through separate account allocations and ongoing performance visibility.

01

Discover

Traders introduce their approach; investors explore the available talent network.

02

Assess

Trading records or a two-month demo evaluation support an approval review.

03

Allocate

Eligible investors choose approved traders and connect eligible exchange subaccounts.

04

Monitor

Both sides review relevant trading activity and risk information through planned dashboards.

FOR TRADERS

A pathway from demonstrated skill to possible backing.

Traders can apply with existing trading records, provide read-only account information for review, or demonstrate their approach through a two-month demo assessment.

  • Apply and describe your experience.
  • Complete the applicable assessment.
  • Become eligible for investor consideration if approved.
  • Trade within agreed risk parameters if selected.

FOR INVESTORS

Build allocations around the traders you choose.

The proposed minimum starting structure involves selecting at least ten approved traders with 1,000 USDT or more allocated to each, giving a starting total of at least 10,000 USDT.

Capital remains in eligible investor-owned exchange subaccounts. Revat would receive defined trading-only API permissions, with withdrawals disabled.
